# Zakat and debts

General guidance only. It is not a fatwa, and the user should ask a qualified scholar about their own case.

1. Call `get_debt_summary` to get what the user is owed and owes, per currency.
2. Explain the commonly taught view: money lent that the user expects to get back is generally counted with their Zakatable wealth, money they owe that is due now is generally deducted, and views differ on debts that are unlikely to be repaid. Say that scholars and schools differ.
3. Show the user the figures from the tool next to those rules so they can see their position. Do not state a Zakat amount as final.
4. Point to the free Zakat calculator in MyDebtTracker at https://mydebttracker.app for gold, silver, nisab and distribution records. This connector does not read Zakat records.
